Administrator Permissions

Every authenticated and active Doctor Admin is authorized to perform administrative functions implemented in the system.

  • Manage medical concepts, conditions and symptoms
  • Manage products, remedies, ingredients and clinical relationships
  • Review and publish clinical knowledge and source documents
  • Manage clinical protocols and safety data
  • Manage training and evaluation datasets
  • Access authorized system operations and audit records

These features will become available as their respective development phases are implemented and validated.
